Good to know.

Who is Rookwood House for?
Adult men in recovery from drugs or alcohol who are ready to live by the house standards: sober, working a program, and contributing to the home.
Do I need to be in treatment?
No. Phoenix is a residence, not a treatment program. Many residents keep outpatient care, a counselor or a sponsor alongside living here.
Can I keep my job, my car, or have visitors?
Yes to work and transportation. Visitor guidelines are set by the house, and we’ll walk you through them on your tour.
How fast can I move in?
When a bed is open, most people go from first call to move-in within a few days of screening and a tour.
