More than a sober house with rules.

Phoenix homes are structured, monitored residences with house leadership, regular testing and set expectations. We’re a residence, not a treatment program, so residents keep their own outpatient care, counselor or sponsor.

i.
Structure
House meetings, testing, chores, and work or school. The habits of recovery become everyday life.
ii.
Accountability
House leadership on site and residents who speak up early when someone is struggling.
iii.
Community
Shared meals, shared responsibility, and peers who understand the work you’re doing.

Clear standards make a home work.

Every resident agrees to them, and every Phoenix house lives by them.

Sober livingDrug and alcohol free, with regular and random testing.
Program workWeekly recovery meetings and a program of your choice.
Work or schoolA job, classes or service, or an active search.
House meetingsA weekly check-in to keep the home running well.
Shared responsibilityChores, curfew, and respect for the home and neighbors.
Peer accountabilityWe speak up early, and we support each other.
